IT SUPPORT & AUTOMATION

Structured troubleshooting. Practical improvements.

My IT Associate role supports daily retail operations through POS and peripheral checks, server EOD/SOD and backup validation, LAN connectivity checks, preventive hardware inspections, ticket follow-up, and software and hardware troubleshooting.

01

Investigate

Gather details about the issue, what happened before it occurred, and any error messages. Clarify the impact on users or operations.

02

Isolate

Reproduce the issue where possible and narrow down the likely root cause. Check relevant application, device, network, or configuration details.

03

Resolve & validate

Apply a suitable fix, test the result, and communicate the outcome. Document findings where useful to help prevent recurrence.

I create small scripts to simplify repetitive tasks and reduce manual effort. Additional sanitized examples can be added as they are prepared for public sharing.
